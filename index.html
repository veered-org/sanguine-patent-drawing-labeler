    Patent Drawing Labeler

    Reference numerals and leader lines for patent drawings, placed in clear space so the leaders stay short and cross as little of the drawing as possible. Then fix anything on the page.
    1. Open the drawing (PDF or image).
    2. Check the figures it found.
    3. Let Claude find the parts, or list them and click each one.
    4. Edit on the page: drag numerals, curve leaders, add arrows, text or white-out.
    5. Download the labeled PDF, an editable PowerPoint and the reference numeral list.
