1Drawing

Open a drawing PDF or image It stays on your computer: nothing is uploaded.

2Figures

Found automatically. In the Figures view, drag on the page to draw a figure (drawing one around several boxes joins them, e.g. an exploded view), drag a box to move it, drag its corner to resize, Del to remove.

3Parts

Type the parts, one per line, with or without a numeral (12 frame tube or frame tube). Then pick a part and click it on the drawing, once in each figure where it shows.

4Place the numerals

Drag a numeral to move it (it then stays put when you place again); drag the dot at the end of a leader to move the tip. Shift-click adds another spot the leader may point to. Del removes the selected label, Ctrl+Z undoes.

5Download

★Let Claude do the clicking

Claude can look at the drawing, name the parts, number them and say where each one is. Download the gridded page images, give them to Claude with the prompt, then paste its answer back here. Check every leader afterwards.

Patent Drawing Labeler

Reference numerals and leader lines for patent drawings, placed in clear space so the leaders stay short and cross as little of the drawing as possible.
  1. Open the drawing (PDF or image).
  2. Check the figures it found.
  3. List the parts and click each one on the drawing, or let Claude do it.
  4. Download the labeled PDF, an editable PowerPoint and the reference numeral list.

Paste Claude’s answer

Paste the whole reply; the JSON inside it is picked out. Parts are added to your list, and each label is snapped to the nearest line and placed.
